# Job Search Criteria

## Roles (any of these)
- Full-stack Developer
- Backend Developer
- Frontend Developer
- Software Engineer (entry level / fresher)
- Internships (SWE / full-stack / backend / frontend)

## Experience level
- FRESHER / ENTRY-LEVEL / JUNIOR only
- 0–1 years experience
- Recent graduate (B.E. AI & Data Science, 2026)
- Internships are welcome

## Locations (priority order)
1. High priority (on-site): Hyderabad, Bengaluru, Pune, Mumbai, Navi Mumbai, Delhi/NCR, Chennai
2. Remote (anywhere): preferred if the role is remote
3. Other India locations / anywhere else: acceptable

## Companies
- Any (startups, product companies, service companies, research orgs)

## Application method (preference)
- Prioritize postings that accept direct email / direct contact (recruiter or hiring/HR email on the job post or careers page)
- Note when a posting requires a portal/ATS form (those cannot be applied to by email — flag them separately)
